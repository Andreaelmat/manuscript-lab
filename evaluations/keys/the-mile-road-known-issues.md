# Known issues: The Mile Road

Answer key for `examples/long-distance/the-mile-road.md`. Do not open this file while producing a review. Open it only after the review and its verification report are finished.

This fixture was written by the same project that maintains the skill. If that same agent reviews the manuscript, the review is contaminated. Do not treat such a review as independent evidence.

The planted factual clashes are far apart on purpose. A review that notices only adjacent chapters, or that calls a resolved letter an open contradiction, has missed the point of the fixture. Lines marked `quote:` are exact excerpts.

## Factual problems

### L1. Flood weekday

- kind: factual
- chapters: 1, 17
- What a review should say: the narration dates 2 May as Friday and later as Thursday. Nothing after chapter 17 corrects the weekday.
- quote: The flood reached the mile road on Friday, 2 May, and took the lower fences before noon.
- quote: The flood had come on Thursday, 2 May, and the road had vanished before noon.

### L2. Scar side

- kind: factual
- chapters: 2, 15
- What a review should say: the same scar is on Mara's left cheek and later on her right cheek. No injury or disguise moves it.
- quote: The scar on Mara's left cheek was white in the cold.
- quote: Mara turned her right cheek to the fire so the scar would warm.

### L3. Blue cup

- kind: factual
- chapters: 4, 6, 16
- What a review should say: Mara leaves the cup and the narration says nothing from the church step came with them. Chapter 16 says she carried that cup since the church.
- quote: She left the blue cup on the church step and did not pick it up again.
- quote: Nothing from the church step came with them.
- quote: Mara drank from the blue cup she had carried since the church

### L4. Age across a short journey

- kind: factual
- chapters: 3, 11, 18
- What a review should say: Mara is nineteen when she leaves. The walk takes nineteen days and contains no birthday. At the coast she is twenty-six, described as the age she had when she left.
- quote: Mara was nineteen.
- quote: The walk from the oak to the coast took nineteen days. No birthday fell inside those days.
- quote: At the coast Mara was twenty-six, the age she had been when she left the village.

### L5. Who told Mara about the bridge

- kind: factual
- chapters: 12, 13, 16
- What a review should say: the narration says nobody told Mara the bridge had fallen, and that they had not seen Osip since the river. Mara then says Osip told her yesterday. Those claims cannot all be true.
- quote: No one had told Mara that the bridge had fallen.
- quote: They had not seen the ferryman since the river.
- quote: Osip told me yesterday that the bridge had fallen

## Not open errors

### L6. Tomas's letter

- kind: resolved
- chapters: 5, 8, 14
- What a review should say: the dead brother and the letter signed Tomas look incompatible until chapter 14. After that chapter the letter is from Lev's living son, who used the dead uncle's name. Do not leave this as an open factual contradiction.
- quote: Tomas drowned in the January ice
- quote: I am at the port. Do not tell the road my name. — Tomas
- quote: The letter was from my son
- quote: He signed his dead uncle's name

### L7. August well

- kind: flashback
- chapters: 7
- What a review should say: the dry well is years before the flood, and the text says that August was not this May. It is not a second date for the flood.
- quote: Years before the flood, in August, the well behind the church had gone dry.
- quote: That August was not this May.

### L8. Grey cloak

- kind: consistent
- chapters: 9, 13, 18
- What a review should say: Osip gives Mara the cloak, she wears it on the road, and she hangs it at the coast. That sequence agrees.
- quote: put a grey cloak over Mara's shoulders
- quote: Mara pulled the grey cloak over her head
- quote: Mara hung the grey cloak on a nail by a hostel door

### L9. Ferry bargain

- kind: strength
- chapters: 10
- What a review should say: Mara wants to cross, Osip wants her name in the book, and neither restates the price. Do not recommend rewriting the scene. Recording it as a strength is appropriate.
- quote: Mara set the cloak's wet hem on the book and waited.

## What this key cannot prove

Finding L1 through L5, refusing L6 as an open error, and leaving L7 through L9 unpunished is the expected shape of one careful full read. It does not show that a review of a different novel will do the same. A contaminated review, by an agent that planted these items, proves less than a review by an agent that has not read this file.
